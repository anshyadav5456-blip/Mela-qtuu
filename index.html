A Note For You 💜

Sach bolun toh... kabhi-kabhi jab main sochta hoon, toh yehi lagta hai ki tumhaari life me kitni hi cheezein chal rahi hoti hain, kitni saari baatein tum apne andar hi daba leti ho.

Par ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna—chahe kuch bhi ho jaye, kitni bhi badi problem ho ya tum kitna bhi overthink kar rahi ho... main hamesha tumhare sath hoon.

Tumhe har choti baat par itna zyada sochna band karna padega, kyunki tumhari yeh pyari si smile kisi bhi cheez se zyada important hai. Thoda relax kiya karo, thoda mujh par bhi chhod diya karo.

Main bas yeh chahta hoon ki tum hamesha aise hi muskuraati raho, khubsurat lagti raho, kyunki tumhara hasta hua chehra dekh kar mera aadha din toh waise hi ban jata hai.

Hamesha aise hi rehna, aur kabhi yeh mat sochna ki tum akeli ho. I'm right here, always. ✨
